## Wrong PIN

If you enter a wrong PIN, the payment or ATM withdrawal is declined.
The app then shows ERR-3007 (wrong PIN).

Try again with the right PIN. Be careful: after 3 wrong PINs, the PIN is blocked.

## Blocked PIN

After 3 wrong PINs, your card PIN is blocked.
The app then shows ERR-3003 (card PIN blocked after 3 wrong PINs).

You can unblock the PIN yourself in the app:

1. Open the app.
2. Go to your card.
3. Unblock the PIN.
4. Use the right PIN for your next payment.

## Keep your PIN secret

- Never tell anyone your PIN.
- Tallybridge staff never ask for your PIN. Not in chat, not by email, not by phone.
- Do not write your PIN in a message to support.

## If someone else knows your PIN

Freeze your card in the app at once. Then contact us. If you see a payment you did not make, report it. You must report unauthorised transactions within 13 months.
