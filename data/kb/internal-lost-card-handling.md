## First instruction at once

For a lost or stolen card, the first instruction is always the same and may come from the AI: **freeze the card in the app now.** After that, a human takes over.

Lost or stolen card is urgent: first human response within 30 minutes. Outside support hours, post the case in `#support-escalations`.

## Steps for the operator

1. Check in the console that the card is frozen. If not, freeze it.
2. Ask the customer if there are card payments they did not make. If yes, follow the fraud checklist and open a fraud case.
3. Explain that a lost or stolen card must be replaced. The replacement is a new delivery with the delivery fee: €9.90 standard or €24.90 express.
4. Tell the customer the delivery time: 7–14 business days in the EU, up to 21 business days elsewhere, express 3–5 business days (EU only).
5. Suggest a virtual card while they wait (free).

Never ask for the full card number, CVV or PIN.

## Waiving the replacement fee

Waiving the delivery fee is a fee refund. Follow the goodwill rules: up to €25 per case by the operator, above that a supervisor approves. Do not offer it by default.

## Card not delivered

A card that has not arrived is not lost until the delivery time has passed.

1. Check the delivery method and the order date.
2. If the time has not passed, explain the delivery time.
3. If the time has passed, freeze the card and discuss a replacement with the customer. Replacing a card that was never delivered is free: do not charge the delivery fee.

## Card found again

If the customer finds the card after a fraud case was opened, do not unfreeze without checking the fraud case.
