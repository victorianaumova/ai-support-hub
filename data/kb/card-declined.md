## Check the error code

When a card payment is declined, the app shows an error code. Find your code below.

## Balance and card status

- **ERR-3001: insufficient balance.** The card spends from your balance. Add money to your balance and try again.
- **ERR-3002: card frozen.** Unfreeze the card in the app.
- **ERR-3004: card cancelled.** This card cannot be used. Use another active card.

## Limits

- **ERR-3011: daily spend limit reached.** The card spend limit is €5,000 per day (Verified) or €15,000 per day (Business).
- **ERR-3012: daily ATM withdrawal limit reached.** The ATM limit is €1,000 per day (Verified) or €2,000 per day (Business).

Daily limits are in EUR equivalent and reset at 00:00 CET.

## PIN problems

- **ERR-3007: wrong PIN.** The PIN you entered at the terminal or ATM was wrong. Try again with the right PIN.
- **ERR-3003: card PIN blocked after 3 wrong PINs.** Unblock the PIN in the app.

## Blocked merchants

**ERR-3005: merchant category blocked.** The card cannot be used for gambling, crypto exchanges or adult content. Use another way to pay.

## Online payments

**ERR-3009: 3-D Secure failed.** The online security check for the payment was not completed. Try the payment again and complete the check.

## Payments in other currencies

A card payment is made in the merchant's currency. If you do not hold that currency, the amount is converted with the conversion fee (2.0%, Business 1.5%). Make sure your balance covers the converted amount.

## Still declined?

If there is no error code, or the code is not in this list, contact us. Tell us the date, the amount and the merchant name. Never send your full card number, CVV or PIN.
