## Limits per tier

| | Starter | Verified | Business |
|---|---|---|---|
| Receive per month | €1,000 | €30,000 | €250,000 |
| Withdraw per day | €500 | €10,000 | €50,000 |
| Withdraw per month | €1,000 | €30,000 | €250,000 |
| Card spend per day | — | €5,000 | €15,000 |
| ATM withdrawal per day | — | €1,000 | €2,000 |

Starter accounts have no card.

## Pending card payments

Pending card payments count toward the daily card spend limit.

## Limits are in EUR

All limits are in EUR equivalent. A payment in USD or GBP counts with its value in EUR.

## When limits reset

- Daily limits reset at 00:00 CET.
- Monthly limits reset on the 1st day of the month.

CET means Central European Time, including summer time (CEST).

## Error codes for limits

- ERR-1020: monthly receive limit reached.
- ERR-2001: daily withdrawal limit reached.
- ERR-2002: monthly withdrawal limit reached.
- ERR-3011: daily card spend limit reached.
- ERR-3012: daily ATM withdrawal limit reached.

## Getting higher limits

- **Starter:** move to the Verified tier. You need proof of address and proof of source of income.
- **Verified and Business:** you can ask for a limit increase. See the article about limit increases.
