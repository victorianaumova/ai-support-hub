## Delivery options and fees

| Option | Fee | Delivery time |
|---|---|---|
| Standard | €9.90 | 7–14 business days in the EU; up to 21 business days elsewhere |
| Express | €24.90 | 3–5 business days (EU only) |

Express delivery is only available in the EU.

## What a business day is

Business days are Monday to Friday, without the closing days of the euro payment system (TARGET calendar).

## While you wait

You can use a virtual card while your physical card is on its way. Virtual cards are free. A Verified account can have 3 virtual cards, a Business account 20.

## My card has not arrived

1. Check which delivery option you chose and the date you ordered the card.
2. Count the business days. Standard delivery takes 7–14 business days in the EU and up to 21 business days elsewhere. Express takes 3–5 business days.
3. If the time has not passed yet, please wait a little longer.
4. If the time has passed, contact us. Tell us the date you ordered the card and your delivery address country. Replacing a card that never arrived is free.

If you think someone else has your card, freeze it in the app at once.

## Replacement cards

When a lost or stolen card is replaced, the replacement is a new delivery. The delivery fee applies: €9.90 standard or €24.90 express.
